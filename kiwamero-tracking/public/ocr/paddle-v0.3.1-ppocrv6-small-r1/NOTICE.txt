Kiwamero browser OCR

Runtime: pure-onnx-ocr v0.3.1, single-threaded WebAssembly
Author: Shion Watanabe / siska-tech and contributors
Source: https://github.com/siska-tech/pure-onnx-ocr
Build distribution: https://siska-tech.github.io/pure-onnx-ocr/v0.3.1/
License declared by upstream: Apache-2.0

Models and recognition dictionary: PaddlePaddle PP-OCRv6 small
Authors: PaddlePaddle / PaddleOCR contributors
Sources:
https://huggingface.co/PaddlePaddle/PP-OCRv6_small_det_onnx
https://huggingface.co/PaddlePaddle/PP-OCRv6_small_rec_onnx
License: Apache-2.0

The generated runtime JavaScript, WASM, models and inference configurations are
distributed without modification. Exact source URLs, sizes and SHA-256 hashes
are recorded in assets.json. The inference configuration includes the original
recognition character dictionary. worker.js is Kiwamero's application adapter,
not the upstream demo worker.

The runtime contains Rust dependencies, including tract, image, imageproc,
geo/i_overlay and wasm-bindgen. Their source and dependency versions are
available through the upstream Cargo.lock and source repository.
